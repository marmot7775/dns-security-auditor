About & Methodology

dns-audit.com is a free tool that checks your domain's email authentication and DNS security configuration. Enter a domain, get a letter grade, and see exactly where you stand. No account required, no data stored.

What We Check

Email Authentication

  • DMARC tells receiving mail servers what to do with messages that fail authentication. Without it, anyone can send email pretending to be you.
  • SPF publishes a list of servers authorized to send email for your domain. Receivers use it to spot fakes.
  • DKIM attaches a cryptographic signature to each email, so receivers can verify the message was not altered in transit.

Email Delivery Security

  • MTA-STS tells other mail servers to use TLS encryption when delivering to you, preventing downgrade attacks.
  • TLS-RPT gives you reports when other servers have trouble delivering over TLS, so you can catch problems early.
  • DANE pins your mail server's TLS certificate in DNS, letting senders verify they are talking to the real server.
  • BIMI displays your brand logo next to your emails in supported clients. It requires a strong DMARC policy to work.

DNS Infrastructure

  • DNSSEC cryptographically signs your DNS records so resolvers can detect spoofed or tampered responses.
  • CAA controls which certificate authorities are allowed to issue TLS certificates for your domain.
  • Nameservers are checked for redundancy, geographic diversity, and proper configuration.
  • Certificate Transparency monitors public CT logs for certificates issued for your domain, helping you spot unauthorized issuance.

Reputation

  • Blacklist Check queries major DNS blocklists to see if your mail servers have been flagged for abuse or spam.

How Scoring Works

Your domain gets a weighted score out of 100, which maps to a letter grade from A through F. DMARC and SPF carry the most weight because they are the foundation of email authentication. Everything else layers on top.

The score reflects how well your domain is actually protected right now. It is not a theoretical risk assessment. If your records are correctly configured and enforced, you score well. If they are missing or misconfigured, the score tells you where to focus.

Standards

All checks are based on current RFCs and best practices.

DMARC analysis supports both the original RFC 7489 (2015, Informational) and the forthcoming DMARCbis specification (draft-ietf-dmarc-dmarcbis, currently in the RFC Editor Queue as a Proposed Standard). DMARCbis will officially replace RFC 7489 once published.

DMARCbis features supported by this tool include the tree walk algorithm (Section 4.10) for policy discovery across the domain hierarchy, the np= tag for non-existent subdomain policy, the t= tag for test mode rollout, the psd= flag for public suffix domains, and deprecation warnings for tags removed in DMARCbis (pct=, ri=, rf=). You can see the tree walk visualized in the audit results.

A note on adoption: The new DMARCbis tags (np=, t=, psd=) are backward-compatible. Receivers that have not yet implemented DMARCbis will simply ignore them. However, the tree walk for policy inheritance is a receiver-side behavior. Not all receivers may have adopted it yet, so a subdomain that inherits a policy via the tree walk may not be fully protected at every receiver today.
